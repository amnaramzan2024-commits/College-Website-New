About Our Institute

GPIW Latifabad is a premier technical education institute committed to providing quality skill-based learning. We focus on modern training and career-focused education to prepare students for future success.

Our Vision

Employment Through Skills

Mission

To provide quality education based on STEVETA regulations in market driven trades to females in safe and healthy environment with strong industrial linkages under trained teaching faculty to produce entrepreneurial oriented skilled workforce which will serve national and international market to uplift socio economic condition of country.

Objectives

To educate women and enable them to attain personal and professional goals. To impart technical skills in women so that they actively contribute to socio economic environment of society. To establish campus environment where trainees learn values, honesty, integrity, judgment and desire for knowledge.

Quality Policy

We are committed to provide continuous competency based training with expert faculty so as to produce high quality trained human resources. We strongly believe to provide relevant, accessible, high quality and efficient technical education and skills development courses to meet the requirement of latest automated industry.

CIT Diploma

Computer Information Technology - 3 Years Program

Course Overview

The Computer Information Technology (CIT) Diploma provides comprehensive training in computer applications, programming, networking, and web development technologies. The program is designed to build strong technical and practical skills required for modern IT industry, enabling students to confidently work in real-world environments.

Career Opportunities

After completing CIT Diploma, students can pursue careers in IT and technology sector as:

Computer Operator
Web Developer
Network Technician / Administrator Assistant
IT Support Assistant
Freelancer (Web, Data Entry, Projects)

Core Subjects (CIT Diploma)

These are main technical subjects that form foundation of IT field:

MS OFFICE
Object Oriented Programming (c, java)
Web Page Development (html,css,javascript)
Graphic Designing (Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, CorelDRAW)
RDBMS (SQL+PHP)
Data Communication & Networking
Networking Administration
Operating System
Software Installation
Hardware Repairing
Digital Electronics
Linear Circuit & Basic Electronics
Analog Electronics

Garments Diploma

Garment Manufacturing Technology - 3 Years Program

Course Overview

The Garment Technology Diploma provides comprehensive training in fashion design, garment production, stitching techniques, and textile science. It focuses on both creative and industrial skills, preparing students for careers in fashion and textile industry.

Career Opportunities

After completing this diploma, students can work as:

Tailor / Professional Stitching Expert
Fashion Designer Assistant
Boutique Worker / Dress Maker
Garment Production Worker
Quality Control Assistant
Freelancer (Fashion & Custom Clothing)

Core Subjects (Garment Technology)

These are main technical subjects that form foundation of textile field:

Pattern Making (I, II, III)
Sewing Technique (I, II, III)
Fashion Designing
Clothing Designing
Garment Machinery & Maintenance
Textile Chemistry
Embroidery
Knitting
Production Planning
Raw Material

Government-Funded Free Skill Training for a Better Future

About Program

NAVTTC offers high-quality, government-funded skill development programs designed to empower students with practical, job-ready abilities. These courses help learners build strong career foundations and become self-reliant in today’s competitive world.

💡 Courses Offered

Digital Marketing

Learn to promote brands online, master social media strategies, SEO, and content creation to grow businesses digitally.

Fashion Designing

Explore your creativity. Learn garment construction, pattern making, and textile science to launch your career in the fashion industry.

Office Automation

Master MS Office and administrative skills. Become proficient in data management, documentation, and office productivity tools.

🚀 What Students Learn

  • Practical, hands-on training with real-world applications.
  • Modern digital and technical skills relevant to current market demands.
  • Freelancing and job-oriented knowledge to earn from home or office.
  • Business and entrepreneurship basics to start your own venture.

🌟 Benefits

  • 100% free training under NAVTTC.
  • Recognized certification upon course completion.
  • Career opportunities in local and international markets.
  • Support for freelancing and self-employment guidance.

Our Laboratories

Advanced facilities for practical learning and skill development

About Our Labs

Government Polytechnic Institute for Women Latifabad provides modern laboratories where students can learn through practical work and real experience. Our labs help students improve their technical skills, creativity, and confidence for future careers. We have separate labs for CIT and Garments departments, equipped with the tools and equipment needed for hands-on training in a professional learning environment.

CIT (Computer Information Technology)

Modern Lab Setup

Fully equipped computer lab for practical training.

Practical Learning

Student focused on system operations and software.

Theory Class

Interactive theoretical sessions in the classroom.

CIT Lab Rules

  • Students should use computers and lab equipment carefully.
  • Internet and computer systems must be used only for educational purposes.
  • Eating and drinking are not allowed inside the computer lab.
  • Students should follow the instructor’s instructions during practical work.
  • Keep the lab clean and properly shut down computers after use.

Garments

Practical Training

Students learning stitching techniques on industrial machines.

Theory Class

Understanding garment concepts and design theory.

Garments Lab Rules

  • Students should handle sewing machines and tools carefully.
  • Keep fabrics, scissors, and equipment organized after practical work.
  • Follow all safety instructions while using machines and cutting tools.
  • Eating and drinking are not allowed inside the garments lab.
  • Maintain cleanliness and return all tools to their proper place after use.

Chemistry Lab

Practical Environment

Modern lab setup for chemical experiments.

Chemistry Lab Rules

  • Always wear a lab coat and safety equipment during practical work.
  • Do not eat, drink, or use mobile phones inside the laboratory.
  • Handle chemicals and glassware carefully.
  • Follow the teacher’s instructions at all times.
  • Keep your work area clean and organized.
  • Report any accident, spill, or damage immediately.
  • Do not touch chemicals without permission.
  • Wash your hands after completing experiments.
